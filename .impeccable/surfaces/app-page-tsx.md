Scope: home page, first viewport. Mode: Persuade. Two equal audiences: people who create with real faces, people who lend their face.

## Direction contract

THESIS: one real certified photo seen from both sides; refuses the category default of a slogan hero with a stock face and a stats row.

OWN-WORLD: ivory ground, near-black ink, amber only on the creator's action, black pill for the person's action, 20px panels (white for the creator, warm hairline-soft for the person), Instrument Sans 700 display, real watermarked photography.

STORY: the visitor understands in one screen that every face said yes, sees the real price and what the person received for the same shot, and picks a door: create or lend the face.

FIRST VIEWPORT: nav; centered headline "Ogni volto qui ha detto sì." at about 79px on a 1536 frame; one grey line under it; two equal panels side by side below: left the certified shot with a "Certificato SEMBLIC" link to Sigil, format and quality, price, amber "Crea con volti veri"; right the portrait, name, "consenso attivo", three lines (what she received, revocation, her own limits), black "Metti il tuo volto". Phone: headline, then the two panels as a sideways swipe, the second peeking.
